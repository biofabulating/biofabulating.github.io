BioFabulating Urban Waters

Sensing, Making and Storytelling with Biomatter

24 January 2027 · 9:00–17:00
BioLab Lisboa
16 places, in person

Call for Participation

Calling all curious people! Whether you are a maker, interaction designer, or nature lover, join us to explore life and once-living matter from the landscapes where the river meets the ocean in the beautiful city of Lisbon. In this in-person, full-day studio, we will combine ecological encounters, hands-on biomaterial-making, speculative fabulation, and tangible and embedded interaction design. Working with locally and responsibly pre-sourced biomatter, including discarded oyster shells and seaweed, we will experiment with transforming biological residues into biomaterials, exploring their properties and potential for interaction.

Through speculative fabulation, we will weave stories around the matter's origins, ecological relationships, and more-than-human worlds. We will then imagine interactive artefacts that bring these stories into tangible form, exploring how we sense, encounter, and relate to them.

Participants will leave with material experiments and speculative design concepts that explore interactions emerging from biomatter's material properties, histories, and ecological relations.

No prior knowledge of biomaterials is required.

BioLab Lisboa is an educational laboratory and makerspace open to the public, offering a practical setting for hands-on experimentation with biomaterials. BioLab is located in a vibrant, multicultural part of Lisbon, offering conference attendees an opportunity to visit a different part of the city. It is easily accessible by public transport and taxi, and has step-free access points.

Programme

The day unfolds in five stages, moving from sensing raw biomatter to imagining interactive artefacts.

01Encounter09:00–09:30

An introduction to (once-living) biomatter and its ecological backgrounds: the Tagus and Sado estuaries, their bioregional practices and ecologies, and non-extractive sourcing. Touch, smell and sense the raw biomatter, and handle prepared biomaterial samples.

02Make09:30–12:00

In groups, prepare a few carefully selected biomaterial recipes using ethically sourced, pre-prepared ingredients.

03Fabulate13:00–15:00

With ideation prompt cards, learn ecological, historical and cultural facts about the once-living matter behind each biomaterial, and shift between human and more-than-human perspectives to build speculative narratives.

04Ideate13:00–15:00

Develop speculative concepts for tangible or embedded artefacts that connect material presence, ecological context and interactive storytelling. The focus is on concepts, not fully functional prototypes.

05Share15:30–16:30

Groups present their artefacts and fabulations, followed by an open discussion on how making with situated biomatter can shape our understandings of sustainability, computation and material agency.
